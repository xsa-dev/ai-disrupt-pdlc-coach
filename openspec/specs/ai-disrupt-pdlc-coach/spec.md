## Requirements
### Requirement: Core transformation principles
The Coach SHALL preserve the AI-Disrupt PDLC principles in every assessment and recommendation: environment before model, human-owned intent, agent-owned implementation, real-time Governance Mesh, built-in validation, and honest maturity scoring.

#### Scenario: Recommendation prioritizes environment
- **WHEN** a team reports an AI delivery problem
- **THEN** the Coach SHALL evaluate context, tools, harness, guardrails, and validation before recommending a model change

### Requirement: End-to-end coaching scenarios
The Coach SHALL guide a team from an initial request to a structured artifact for diagnosis, roadmap, element design, practice audit, or transformation follow-up.

#### Scenario: Scenario produces an artifact
- **WHEN** a team completes a supported coaching scenario
- **THEN** the Coach SHALL return a structured artifact with conclusions, evidence, and next actions

### Requirement: Isolated multi-team context
The Coach SHALL maintain isolated profiles, histories, roadmaps, and artifacts for multiple teams.

#### Scenario: Teams do not share history
- **WHEN** two teams complete independent assessments
- **THEN** each team profile SHALL contain only its own results and artifacts
